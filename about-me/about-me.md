Hi, I am Alex! An evolutionary biologist with a passion for creating art.

After graduating from high school, I did a year of ecological voluntary service (FÖJ) at the UNESCO World Heritage Wadden Sea. Afterwards, I completed my BSc in Bio Sciences at the University of Rostock and my MSc in Biological Oceanography at Kiel University and GEOMAR. I stayed at GEOMAR for my PhD working on salinity adaptation in the copepod *Acartia tonsa*. 

Throughout my career, I have always tried to find ways to combine art with science.
