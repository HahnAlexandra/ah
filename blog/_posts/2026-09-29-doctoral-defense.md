# I defended my PhD thesis!

On September 29, I successfully defended my doctoral thesis titled "Integrating physiology, transcriptomics, and seascape genomics to study adaptation in the copepod *Acartia tonsa* along the Baltic Sea salinity gradient".

In my project, I combined molecular methods with lab experiments to show how *A. tonsa* has adapted to the low salinity conditions in the Baltic and assess the species' resilience to future salinity changes. My work highlights the importance of integrative assessments and demonstrates the utility of *A. tonsa* as a model system for local adaptation.
