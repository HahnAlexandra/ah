# Cover Art

Look at these copepods! For my doctoral thesis 'Integrating physiology, transcriptomics, and seascape genomics to study adaptation in the copepod *Acartia tonsa* along the Baltic Sea salinity gradient', I wanted to create a cover image that would reflect the study organism, the study system and reference the molecular methods that I used.
