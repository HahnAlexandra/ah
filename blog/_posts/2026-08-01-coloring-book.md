# Baltic Sea Coloring Book

In collaboration with Katja Sebass (pictured on the right) and the Communication & Media Team at GEOMAR, we developed the coloring book "Ein Blick in die Ostsee" (English translation: A glimpse into the Baltic Sea). In 32 hand-illustrated pages children meet the inhabitants of the Baltic Sea and learn about them drawing by drawing. From microscopic phytoplankton to marine mammals, grab you colored pencils and dive in!

From now on, the coloring book is also available for download on the GEOMAR website under [**Publications**](https://www.geomar.de/en/discover/publications). It is not only suitable for families, but can also be used as a teaching material for school lessons. 

Download now, get out your crayons and discover the Baltic Sea!
