## Pencil and Pens

![Lifecycle](/assets/img/projects/carcinus_lifecycle.jpg)

Here, I depicted the life stages of the common green crab (*Carcinus maenas*). From eggs hatch planktonic zoea larvae, that develop into megalopa larvae. Only at the end of this stage do the larvae find their way to the sea floor and molt into juvenile green crabs that will spend the rest of their life as part of the benthic (bottom-dwelling) community. Black pen on paper.
{:.note}

## Watercolor

In this artistic interpretation of a marine food web the watercolors add the impression of flowing water. Featuring various phytoplankton species as primary producers, zooplankton represented by copepods and cladocerans as primary consumers, and two herring as secondary consumers.Watercolor on paper.
{:.note}
