This coloring book was created in collaboration with the Communication & Media Team at GEOMAR. In 32 hand-illustrated pages children meet the inhabitants of the Baltic Sea and learn about them drawing by drawing. From microscopic phytoplankton to marine mammals, grab you colored pencils and dive in!

For this project, I contributed 15 pages and the front cover illustration.

Below you see an example page featuring the all the tiny organisms that you normally would not see this way.
